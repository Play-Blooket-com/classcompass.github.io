Notes

How is the grade worked out?

Each score times its weight, added up, then divided by the weights that have a score. With the numbers already in the table that's (88 × 40 + 95 × 25 + 82 × 20) / 85 = 88.65. Participation has no score yet, so its 15% isn't counted.

What scale does the letter use?

90, 80, 70 and 60 for A through D, with a minus below x3 and a plus from x7 up. So 88.65 is a B+. Your school might draw the lines somewhere else.

Why 25 minutes?

That's the Pomodoro technique, from Francesco Cirillo. The lengths are fixed on this page, so if 25 is wrong for you, use Reset and stop whenever you like.

MLA or APA?

Whichever your teacher asked for. MLA tends to show up in English and other humanities classes, APA in psychology and the social sciences. If the author and the website are the same name, APA leaves the website out.

Do my cards follow me to another computer?

No. They stay in the browser you made them in.
